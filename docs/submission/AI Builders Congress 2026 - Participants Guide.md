# **1\. Vision & Strategic Intent**

The International AI Builders Congress 2026 is not a conference, and it is not a hackathon. It is a six-month builder journey designed to transform participants into world-class AI product builders capable of creating globally scalable products, venture-backed startups, intellectual property, jobs, and measurable economic value.

From July through December, participants learn, build, collaborate, mentor, validate, and launch solutions with the potential to become globally competitive businesses. The journey culminates on December 26, 2026, when more than 5,000 builders gather at Daffodil International University to showcase production-ready innovations, compete, and connect with investors and employers.

The Congress exists to answer one question: how do we develop the next generation of builders who can create products that compete globally and solve meaningful problems? It brings universities, industry leaders, and global practitioners onto one platform so that ideas move beyond prototypes toward production-grade solutions with real users, measurable impact, and international relevance.

**Strategic Goal:** Position Bangladesh not merely as a user of artificial intelligence, but as a trusted source of AI-native builders, teams, and solutions for the global economy.

# **2\. Why This Matters Now**

## **The AI Shift Is Already Happening**

The world is in the middle of the fastest technological transition since the internet. Artificial intelligence has moved from research labs into everyday economic infrastructure. Within just a few years, AI systems have become capable of writing software, generating multimedia content, analyzing massive datasets, and automating complex decision-making.

Global analysts estimate AI could contribute more than US$15 trillion to the global economy by 2030, while companies rapidly restructure around AI-native product teams that combine developers, domain experts, and intelligent systems to build solutions faster than traditional methods. Multimodal models that understand text, images, audio, and video simultaneously are already transforming healthcare, finance, education, agriculture, and commerce. At the same time, misinformation, deepfakes, and data manipulation make trustworthy, sovereign AI increasingly critical for societies and institutions worldwide.

## **Why the Timing Matters for Bangladesh**

Bangladesh has a population of over 170 million people, more than half under the age of 30, with millions studying in universities, colleges, polytechnics, and technical training programs. Every year hundreds of thousands of graduates enter a rapidly digitizing global economy. Meanwhile, thousands of Non-Resident Bangladeshi (NRB) professionals already work at leading global technology companies, startups, and research institutions.

The opportunity is clear: if Bangladesh can connect its young talent with global expertise and train them to build real AI systems — not just learn about them — it can become a major contributor to the global digital workforce. The challenge is that most local innovation still focuses on adopting technology rather than producing it. Countries that develop the ability to build and deploy AI solutions will define the next generation of economic leadership.

The Congress creates a platform where Bangladeshi builders, NRB technologists, universities, and industry leaders work together to develop AI-native products that compete globally — catalyzing a national shift from AI adoption to AI production.

# **3\. The Six-Month Builder Journey**

The Congress is structured as a continuous, milestone-driven build journey rather than a single event day. Participants progress from onboarding and skills-building through prototyping, validation, production hardening, and final submission, ending in the December 26 Global Showcase. Each phase has clear deliverables that compound into a production-ready product and an investor-ready story.

| Phase | Window | Focus | Key Deliverables |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Phase 0 — Launch & Onboarding | Jul 1 – Jul 31 | Registration opens; domain selection and team formation (or solo entry for Zero to Launch) | Registered profile, selected domain & challenge, formed team with roles assigned |
| Phase 1 — Learn & Design | Aug 1 – Aug 31 | AI-native foundations, architecture design, prompt & context engineering; preliminary submissions open | Problem statement, architecture blueprint, data & sovereignty plan |
| Phase 2 — Build & Prototype | Sep 1 – Sep 30 | Build the AI core, knowledge layer (RAG), agents, and a working vertical slice | Working prototype, demo video v1, KPI definition |

# **5\. The Two Flagship Competitions**

The International AI Builders Congress 2026 is built around two transformational competitions designed to develop the next generation of AI-native builders, founders, innovators, and entrepreneurs. Together, they represent the complete journey of modern innovation: from a single idea, to a launched product, to a globally scalable company.

Every participant competes in one or both. Idea to Unicorn is the flagship team-based competition; Zero to Launch is the signature individual single-prompt challenge, open to anyone. They run on independent workflows and timelines and are scored separately.

| Dimension | Competition 1 — Idea to Unicorn | Competition 2 — Zero to Launch |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Participation** | Team-based (3–5 members \+ 1 Virtual NRB) | Individual — open to all registered participants |
| **Workflow** | Six-month build journey | Preliminary (Oct 15 – Nov 15\) \+ live one-hour final |
| **Objective** | Build a production-ready AI product within a chosen global domain | From ONE master prompt, generate a complete, public, enterprise-grade application ecosystem |
| **Submission** | Full submission form \+ demo video \+ live demo | One master prompt \+ a publicly accessible application, documentation, and audit |
| **Final round** | Structured demo to judging panels | Live, timed, one-hour build on Event Day |
| **Focus** | Collaborative innovation, depth, scale, and impact | Prompt mastery, AI-native generation, public & enterprise readiness |

## **From Idea to Unicorn — Build a Company (I2U) : Team/Company**

Idea to Unicorn is the flagship team-based competition focused on building the next generation of AI-native products, startups, and companies. Over a six-month journey, teams transform ideas into production-ready products by leveraging artificial intelligence, modern software engineering, agentic systems, cloud platforms, automation, and AI-native product development methodologies.

Participants move beyond prototypes and hackathon projects to build real products capable of solving meaningful problems, generating revenue, creating jobs, attracting investment, and scaling globally. The competition challenges teams to think like founders, product leaders, engineers, designers, operators, and entrepreneurs — building solutions that are commercially viable, globally scalable, secure, responsible, and ready for real-world deployment.

The ultimate goal is not simply to build an application. It is to build the next generation of companies that can create economic value, improve lives, and potentially become future unicorns.

**Idea to Unicorn is where builders become founders.**

## **From Zero to Launch — Build a Product (ZTL) : Individual/Product**

Zero to Launch is the signature individual AI-native product engineering challenge. Participants are given a challenge and must transform a single prompt into a working, production-ready application within a highly constrained timeframe. It represents the future of software engineering, where builders leverage AI agents, memory systems, workflows, knowledge bases, MCP integrations, automation frameworks, and modern AI-native development tools to rapidly create complete solutions.

Zero to Launch is not about writing the most code — it is about orchestrating intelligence. Participants are evaluated on their ability to create complete product ecosystems — applications, administration portals, data systems, security frameworks, governance controls, documentation, audit capabilities, architecture, and business readiness — through a single master prompt. The challenge demonstrates how the next generation of builders will create products faster, smarter, and at a scale never before possible.

**Zero to Launch is where builders prove they can transform intelligence into execution.**

# **7\. AI Development Workflow & Reference Architecture**

This workflow guides teams in transforming ideas into deployable AI systems — not just prototypes. It mirrors how modern AI startups and enterprise teams actually build software.

## **Recommended Build Process for AI-Native Applications**

| Phase | Activities | Tools / Technologies | Outcome |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Problem Definition | Define the user problem, audience, and measurable impact; identify why AI is essential | Research tools, LLM brainstorming (Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini) | Clear problem statement & solution concept |
| Architecture Design | Design input data, AI layers, storage, outputs; define agents and workflow | Cursor / Claude Code with markdown architecture files | Documented system blueprint |
| Prompt Engineering | Design structured prompts controlling reasoning, output format, and retrieval | Lovable, Cursor, Claude Code | Reliable, efficient AI responses |
| Knowledge Layer | Build contextual intelligence with RAG; store documents and embeddings | Vector DBs (Supabase / PGVector) or Graph DBs | Context-aware reasoning |

# **11\. Official Judging Framework (Idea to Unicorn)**

Idea to Unicorn is evaluated through a unified, institutional-grade framework that measures innovation, technical depth, scalability, real-world impact, responsible AI, data sovereignty, commercial viability, and presentation clarity. Each team is scored across five components, each worth 20 points, for a total of 100\. The eight Congress evaluation themes — innovation, technical excellence, business value, commercial viability, global scalability, real-world impact, responsible AI, and data sovereignty — are combined into these five components, with presentation assessed throughout.

| Component (20 pts each) | What Judges Evaluate |

Each component is scored out of 20 using the defined bands below, ensuring fairness and consistency across all panels.

| Score Band (of 20\) | Interpretation |

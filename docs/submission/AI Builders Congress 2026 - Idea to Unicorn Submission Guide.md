# **Phase 1 — Preliminary Submission Opens**

**August 1, 2026 — 12:00 PM BST**

This is when the preliminary submission window opens for Idea to Unicorn teams, marking the start of Phase 1 (“Learn & Design”) in the six-month journey. Nothing is due on this date itself — it's your green light to start assembling the package you'll submit by October 31\.

**What your submission package must eventually cover (Section 9.1)**

* Problem Definition — the problem, target users, and real-world need addressed

* AI Architecture — system architecture: input sources, models, pipelines, outputs

* Data & Sovereignty Strategy — data sources, preparation, privacy, governance, ownership

* Demo (Live or Recorded) — a working demonstration in a real user scenario

* Scalability Roadmap — how the system scales beyond prototype: infra, deployment, roadmap

* Ethical Safeguards — bias mitigation, privacy protection, responsible-AI design

# **Phase 2 — Preliminary Submission Deadline**

**October 31, 2026 — 12:00 PM BST**

This is the hard deadline. Every team must submit a 3-minute (180-second) structured video pitch — this is what determines whether your team advances to the Congress finals.

**Required video structure (Section 9.2)**

| Time | Segment | What to Show |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 0:00–0:30 | **Problem** | Define the problem, users, and urgency (local \+ global context) |
| 0:30–1:00 | **Solution** | Your AI-driven solution and its differentiation |
| 1:00–2:00 | **Demo / Flow** | Early prototype, mock flow, or system walkthrough |
| 2:00–2:30 | **AI Approach** | Models, RAG, data, personalization, tools |
| 2:30–3:00 | **Impact & Next Step** | Value, potential users, and what comes next |

**Mandatory requirements checklist**

* A 3-minute structured video pitch

* Clear problem and user definition

* An AI-native approach (LLM / RAG / Graph / ML)

* Basic system flow (input → AI → output)

* An initial demo, prototype, or workflow (early-stage acceptable)

* Consideration for Bangla / localization

* Defined potential impact (KPIs or expected outcomes)

**Common mistakes to avoid**

* Submitting only an idea with no system thinking

* No clear AI explanation

* Overcomplicating instead of focusing on one core use case

* No demo or workflow visualization

* Ignoring real users or real problems

| *Reminder from the Guide: you're not judged on completion — you're judged on your ability to execute. Keep it clear, real, and buildable.* |

# **Phase 3 — Preliminary Selection Announcement**

**November 15, 2026**

CloudCamp Bangladesh / MillionX Bangladesh announce which teams are selected as finalists based on the preliminary submissions. There is nothing for you to submit on this date — it's purely a results/notification milestone. If your team is selected, you move into Phase 4 (Selection & Hardening) to productionize the build ahead of Congress Day.

# **If Selected: The Final Phase Ahead**

**Congress Day Final — December 26, 2026**

Not one of the three preliminary dates above, but worth having on your radar: if your team advances, you'll present live at Daffodil International University (Ashulia Campus, Dhaka).

* Format: 5 minutes per team — 1 minute transition \+ 3 minutes pitch \+ 1 minute scoring

* Structure: problem → solution → demo → AI → impact, followed by judge questions

* Evaluated on: Innovation, AI Implementation, Technical Execution, User Experience, Business Viability, Impact & Scalability
